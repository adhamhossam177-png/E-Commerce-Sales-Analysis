# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using **Python and Pandas** to identify sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and regional performance.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful **business insights** that can support better decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Clean and prepare the sales data.
* Analyze revenue across different products.
* Compare products based on units sold.
* Analyze category performance.
* Identify monthly revenue trends.
* Compare revenue across regions.
* Identify high-value customers.
* Extract meaningful business insights from the data.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab
* Data Cleaning
* Data Analysis
* GroupBy & Aggregation

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### Product Performance

Analyzed products based on total revenue and number of units sold.

### Category Performance

Compared total revenue generated by different product categories.

### Monthly Sales

Analyzed monthly revenue to identify the highest-performing month.

### Regional Performance

Compared revenue generated across different regions.

### Customer Analysis

Identified customers who generated the highest revenue.

### Sales Quantity

Analyzed the number of units sold for each product.

## 💡 Business Insights

* **Laptop** generated the highest revenue among all products.
* **Tablet** had the highest number of units sold.
* **Electronics** generated the highest total revenue among categories.
* **Cairo** generated the highest total revenue among regions.
* **Laila** was the highest-revenue customer.
* **January** recorded the highest monthly revenue.
* The product with the highest sales volume was not necessarily the product with the highest revenue.

## 📌 Conclusion

This project analyzed e-commerce sales data using **Python and Pandas**.

The analysis covered data cleaning, revenue analysis, product performance, category performance, monthly sales, regional performance, customer analysis, and sales quantity.

The results show that revenue performance is influenced not only by the number of units sold, but also by **product prices and purchasing behavior**.

This project demonstrates how data analysis can transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.
